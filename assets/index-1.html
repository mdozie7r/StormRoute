How to play

1 — Pick a deck.
Choose a category and how long the round lasts.
2 — Phone on your forehead.
Hold it in landscape, screen facing your friends.
3 — They give clues.
Guess the word. Tilt the phone DOWN when you get it. Tilt UP to skip.
No tilt sensor?
Tap the right half of the screen for "got it", the left half to pass.
